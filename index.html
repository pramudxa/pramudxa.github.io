01 — About

Engineering through design.

I am an Industrial Engineering student and Design Drafter with nearly three years of experience in the die-making industry. Through my work, I have gained practical experience in CAD, technical drawing, engineering design, and supporting manufacturing processes.

Working in a die-making environment has helped me understand how a design moves from technical drawings into actual manufacturing. I enjoy working with details, dimensions, and design requirements while learning how different engineering decisions affect the manufacturing process.

Alongside my professional experience, I am continuing my studies in Industrial Engineering to broaden my understanding of engineering, manufacturing, and process improvement. I aim to keep developing my technical skills and gain a deeper understanding of how design and manufacturing work together.
